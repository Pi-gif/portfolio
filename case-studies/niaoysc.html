CASE STUDY · NIAOYSC PUBLICITY & CSR

Building a working media-outreach pipeline from the ground up.

As Head of Media for the Nigerian Institute of Architects, Oyo State Chapter, Publicity & CSR Committee, I support media relations and partnership outreach across Oyo State.

ROLE

Head of Media

The challenge
Media outreach required more than a list of names. Contact details needed verification, inactive channels had to be identified and conversations had to be followed through.

What I did
Helped compile and verify a media database, contacted outlets through available channels, followed up on responses and discussed possible partnership models without assuming a particular commercial arrangement.

Documented progress
The team reported reaching approximately 50 media houses and establishing collaboration with several outlets within the initial outreach period. This figure is presented as internal project reporting, not independently verified audience reach.

OUTREACH PIPELINE

Build

Compile relevant newspapers, radio, television and online outlets.

Verify

Check whether contact details and social channels are active.

Pitch

Explain the committee’s work and explore useful partnership models.

Follow up

Track responses, requirements and next steps for each outlet.
