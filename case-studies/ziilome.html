CASE STUDY · ZIILOME

Turning outreach into a more organised vendor onboarding process.

Ziilome is a furniture and interior design discovery platform connecting sellers and buyers. I supported marketing, customer communication and vendor relations.

ROLE

Marketing, Social Media & Brand Support

The challenge
A marketplace needs both sides of the network. My work focused on reaching potential sellers, supporting onboarding and keeping communication organised.

What I did
Managed enquiries across Instagram, Facebook, Threads and email; conducted outreach; followed up with vendors; gathered customer feedback; and supported digital engagement.

Documented result
CV evidence records an approximately 50% increase in vendor sign-ups through structured outreach and digital engagement campaigns.

WORKFLOW

Research

Identify relevant sellers and understand their fit for the platform.

Outreach

Use direct digital communication to introduce the platform and start conversations.

Onboarding

Coordinate information and follow-ups needed to bring vendors onto the platform.

Feedback

Capture customer and vendor feedback for product and experience improvements.
